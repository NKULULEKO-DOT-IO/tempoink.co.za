Complete clean white theme for TEMPOINK temporary tattoo e-commerce store, inspired by ezink.co.za design.

- **Clean White Background** - Professional modern design
- **TEMPOINK Branding** - Custom logo and brand messaging

- **Charcoal Navigation** - `#444444` color for better visibility
